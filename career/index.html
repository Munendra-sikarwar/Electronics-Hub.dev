Career Guidance

Career Roadmap for Electronics and ECE Students

Plan your four years, build high-impact projects, and align your skills with embedded, VLSI, IoT, robotics, and PCB opportunities.

ECE Student Roadmap

Build strong fundamentals in years 1-2, then specialize through domain projects, internships, and portfolio outcomes in years 3-4.

Skills by Year (1st to 4th Year)

1st Year: circuit basics. 2nd Year: analog/digital design. 3rd Year: embedded and communication systems. 4th Year: specialization and placement readiness.

Embedded Systems

Firmware architecture, real-time constraints, and board-level debugging.

VLSI

Digital design flow, timing closure basics, and verification mindset.

IoT

Sensor integration, wireless communication stacks, and cloud handshakes.

Robotics

Perception-control integration with actuator and power reliability focus.

PCB Design

Schematic capture, layer planning, routing, and DFM-aware handoff.

Industry Tools

LTspice, KiCad, MATLAB, Altium, Git, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers.
